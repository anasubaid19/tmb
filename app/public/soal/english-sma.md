# English Diagnostic Test — SMA

**Format:** Interview · **Duration:** 10–15 minutes per student
**Purpose:** To test fluency, critical thinking, and Islamic application.

## Instruction for Interviewers

1. Start with the Islamic greeting (Assalamualaikum warahmatullahi wabarakatu) and a warm smile. Create a relaxed atmosphere first.
2. Deliver each question clearly at moderate pace. Check understanding before moving on.
3. After finishing, give a few words of motivation and close friendly.
4. Fill in the score sheet carefully (Fluency, Vocabulary & Grammar, Critical Thinking, Expression & Clarity — each 1–5).

## 1. Introduction

- Assalamu'alaikum, can you tell me about yourself?
- What motivates you to study at Al-Wildan Islamic School?

## 2. Personal Reflection

- What is one challenge you faced in school, and how did you overcome it?
- Which positive habit do you think is the hardest to practice, and why?

## 3. Islamic Application

- The Prophet (peace be upon him) said: "The best of you are those who learn the Qur'an and teach it." How does this apply to your life as a student?
- If you become a leader in your class, how will you practice fairness ('adl) and responsibility (amanah)?

## 4. Critical Thinking

- Some students feel stressed about exams. What advice would you give them?
- What can you say to young people in facing modern challenges (technology, social media)?

## 5. Explaining

Choose one topic and speak for 2–3 minutes:

- a) My role as a Muslim student in today's world.
- b) A story when I did social service (school, mosque, or neighborhood).
- c) A person I admire and why (Prophet, CEO, or family member).

> Give students time to explore answers, don't interrupt. Follow-up prompts: "Can you give me an example?" or "Why do you think so?"
