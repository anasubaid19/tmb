# English Diagnostic Test — SMP

**Format:** Interview · **Duration:** 10–15 minutes per student
**Purpose:** To check speaking skills, confidence, and Islamic values.

## Instruction for Interviewers

1. Start with salam (Assalamualaikum warahmatullahi wabarakatu) and a warm smile. Create a relaxed atmosphere first.
2. Deliver each question clearly at moderate pace. Check understanding before moving on.
3. After finishing, give a few words of motivation and close friendly.
4. Fill in the score sheet carefully (Fluency, Vocabulary & Grammar, Content — each 1–5).

## 1. Greeting & Introduction

- Assalamu'alaikum, can you introduce yourself?
- What is your favorite subject at school?

## 2. Daily Life

- What do you usually do after school?
- How often do you help your parents at home?

## 3. Islamic Values Application

- How much time do you read the Qur'an at home in each day?
- How do you practice honesty (sidq) in your daily life?
- Why do you think respect for teachers (adab) is important?

## 4. General Conversation

- What do you like to do in your free time?
- If you have a problem with your friend, what will you do?

## 5. Description

Choose one topic. Speak in 1–2 minutes:

- a) My Favorite Toy
- b) My Pet
- c) My Future Dream

> Give students time to explore answers, don't interrupt. Follow-up prompts: "Can you give me an example?" or "Why do you think so?"
